# Code of conduct

Tadabut is a small project, and I want everyone who takes part in it to feel welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient.
- Respect people who disagree with you. Talk about the code, not the person.
- Give feedback that helps. Say what's wrong and, if you can, how to fix it.
- Accept feedback on your own work without taking it personally.
- If you made a mistake, own it and move on.

## What's not okay

- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any other place where you represent the project.

## Reporting a problem

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer, [@ayem1412](https://github.com/ayem1412), through the email on his GitHub profile. You can also open an issue if you're comfortable doing it in public.

Every report will be read and treated seriously. Your name will be kept private if you ask.

## What can happen

Depending on how serious it is, the maintainer may:

1. Send a private warning.
2. Remove comments, commits, or other content.
3. Ban someone from the project for a while, or for good.
